Current Research

Graduate Research Fellow, Cosine Lab, The Ohio State University (Advisor: Prof. Kannan Athreya)

  • Physics-Guided Machine Learning for 5G NR Uplink CSI Prediction

    Uplink sounding reference signals (SRS) give the base station channel state information (CSI), but sounding the full band costs airtime. I am developing a physics-guided, generalizable model that predicts full-band CSI from partial-band SRS. It is trained only on a physics-based digital twin of synthetic channels and learns physical channel parameters rather than acting as a black box. To evaluate it on real channels, I built a 5G NR PHY measurement pipeline on srsRAN and OCUDU (O-RAN 7.2 split) with USRP SDRs, and collected wideband SRS/CSI datasets across indoor, lobby and rooftop environments on the POWDER testbed.

  • Wi-Fi 7 Wireless Sensing for Healthcare

    Building a Wi-Fi sensing system on Wi-Fi 7 (802.11be) access points that collects CSI from everyday commercial devices across the 2.4, 5 and 6 GHz bands at up to 160 MHz. The system is being deployed in a hospital study for contactless seizure detection, and I am designing deep learning models for CSI-based sensing of human activity and gestures.

  • Over-the-Air Federated Learning on a Real 5G Cellular Setup

    Designing a multi-UE over-the-air computation (AirComp) physical layer on a sub-6 GHz 5G NR setup with SDRs and commercial UEs, so that devices train locally and aggregate model updates over the air. This includes OFDM synchronization and channel estimation that keep independently clocked UEs phase-coherent without a shared clock, including under mobility, and studying airtime overhead and spectrum sharing with a live 5G cell.

Publications

  1. Efficient Defense Against First Order Adversarial Attacks on Convolutional Neural Networks

    Authors: Sadia Afrose, Subah Karnine, Dr. Hafiz Imtiaz

    Published at: Artificial Intelligence and Applications, 2025

    Proposed a computational-efficient approach to enhance the robustness of machine learning models against adversarial attacks, where inputs are purposefully altered to induce incorrect predictions. Focused on identifying the parts of the model most vulnerable to adversarial attacks and selectively trained only these sensitive components. This approach achieved equivalent results comparable to conventional full-model adversarial training while using fewer resources, ensuring significant computational load savings.

  2. Aquatic Robot for Trash Removal and Water Quality Monitoring Built from Recycled Materials

    Authors: S.K. Das, S. Afrose, S. Saha, R. Mashtura, F. Fardaous

    Published at: 13th International Conference on Electrical and Computer Engineering (ICECE 2024) · 🏆 Best Paper Award

    Developed an aquatic robot designed to remove trash and monitor water quality. The robot was built using recycled materials, promoting sustainability and efficient use of resources. The system successfully demonstrated its functionality in addressing environmental challenges related to water pollution.
